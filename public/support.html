Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do I move my data between Web and iOS? Open Settings → Backup, tap Export JSON on one device, then tap Import JSON (or paste from clipboard) on the other.
  • How do I pick which openings appear in my Daily Coach? On the Openings tab, tap the Daily pill or press & hold any opening card — or tap 1. Openings · Edit on the Train screen.
  • Does BrillyDer work offline? Yes! The native chess engine, opening courses, mistake drills, Woodpecker cycles, and endgame drills all run 100% on-device offline.
