Brilly/Der Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 24, 2026 · Applies to BrillyDer iOS & BrillyDer Web PWA

Short Version: BrillyDer is 100% local-first. We do not run developer servers, we do not require accounts, we do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not collect, sell, or share any personal data.

1. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

All analyzed games, spaced-repetition (FSRS) opening schedules, mistake decks, bookmarks, board preferences, and custom PGN courses are stored exclusively on your device (in the app's private local container on iOS or IndexedDB in your browser). You can export or delete your local data at any time inside Settings.

2. Optional Third-Party Chess API Requests

BrillyDer only contacts external services when you explicitly use optional online lookup features:

  • Lichess (lichess.org) and Chess.com (api.chess.com): When you tap Sync or Scout with a public chess username, your device connects directly over HTTPS to fetch publicly available game PGNs.
  • Lichess Syzygy Tablebase (tablebase.lichess.ovh): When analyzing or drilling endgames with 7 or fewer pieces on the board, your device may query the public tablebase API with the board position (FEN) for exact win/draw/loss lookup.

No developer proxy sits between your device and these public chess APIs.

3. Device Permissions

  • Photo Library (Add Photos Only): Requested solely if you tap Save Photo to export a move highlight Share Card to your Camera Roll. BrillyDer never reads your existing photo library.

4. Analytics, Advertising & Tracking

BrillyDer contains zero third-party analytics SDKs, zero advertising SDKs, and performs zero cross-app tracking (NSPrivacyTracking = false).

5. Contact & Support

For privacy questions or support requests, visit our Support Page or open an issue on GitHub.
