Brilly/Der Terms of Use

Effective Date: November 24, 2026 · Applies to BrillyDer iOS & BrillyDer Web PWA

Summary: BrillyDer offers a generous Free tier plus optional Brilly/Der Pro upgrades (Monthly, Annual with a 7-day free trial for eligible new subscribers, or a one-time Lifetime purchase) processed securely through Apple's App Store.

1. Brilly/Der Pro Subscriptions & Lifetime Purchase

  • Monthly Subscription (com.brillyder.pro.monthly): Auto-renewable 1-month subscription unlocking all Pro features.
  • Annual Subscription (com.brillyder.pro.annual): Auto-renewable 1-year subscription. Eligible first-time subscribers receive a 7-day free trial before the first annual charge.
  • Lifetime Unlock (com.brillyder.pro.lifetime): One-time non-consumable purchase that permanently unlocks all Brilly/Der Pro features on your Apple Account.

2. Billing, Auto-Renewal & Cancellation

Payment is charged to your Apple Account upon confirmation of purchase (or at the conclusion of the 7-day free trial for eligible Annual subscriptions). Subscriptions automatically renew unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your Apple Account will be charged for renewal within 24 hours prior to the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in your iOS device's Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. Unused portions of a free trial period, if offered, are forfeited when purchasing a subscription.

3. Restoring Purchases

You can restore eligible Pro subscriptions and Lifetime purchases on any iOS device signed into the same Apple Account by tapping Restore Purchases inside BrillyDer's Settings or Upgrade screen.

4. Local-First Software & Third-Party Services

BrillyDer runs chess analysis and spaced-repetition scheduling locally on your device. Optional online lookups (Lichess, Chess.com, and Lichess Syzygy Tablebase) connect directly from your device to those independent third-party services and are subject to their respective availability and terms.
